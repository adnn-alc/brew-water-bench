# Brew Water Bench

A single-page reference for building coffee water from distilled or RO water.

- **Start here**: three steps for a first batch of water
- **Map + mixer**: published recipes plotted by hardness (GH) and alkalinity (KH), and a mixer that turns a target into grams of your own mineral concentrates
- **Concentrate builder**: check the strength and dose of a bottle before making it
- **Minerals**: what magnesium, calcium, sodium, potassium, bicarbonate, chloride and sulfate each do
- **By coffee**: starting points by origin, process and roast, with the basis for each
- **Espresso machine**: scale, corrosion and the rpavlis recipe
- **Products**: what is in Lotus, Apax Lab and Third Wave Water

Live site: https://adnn-alc.github.io/brew-water-bench/

## Running it

There is no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static file server.

## Accuracy

Ion and gram figures are calculated from molar masses and checked against the published recipes. Expected TDS meter readings are estimates from ionic conductivities, good to roughly 10%. Taste claims belong to the sources listed at the bottom of the page. Recipes and pairings are starting points, not prescriptions.
